Founder & Her Vision

“If you have a dream, it is achievable, unbound by time.”

In 2010, as a master’s student with no funds even for my first degree, I got my first passport and took my first flight, an all-expenses-paid trip to Europe. That journey opened the door to more opportunities, and in 2016 I earned my PhD on a full scholarship from Germany’s Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF). I know what one open door can do. That is why I founded ASCENDE: to Edit, Equip and Empower women to find their own doors and walk through them, at any age and from any starting point.
